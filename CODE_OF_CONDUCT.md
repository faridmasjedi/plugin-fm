# Code of Conduct

NomadSF follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer privately through GitHub ([@faridmasjedi](https://github.com/faridmasjedi)). Reports are handled in
confidence, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break this code.
