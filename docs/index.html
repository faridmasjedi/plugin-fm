Your Salesforce workbench, right in the browser

Execute Apex, SOQL, code, debug logs, metadata and org compare — plus data backup, restore, migration and lifecycle jobs. It uses the Salesforce login you already have in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install on the org, nothing sent anywhere but Salesforce.

Everything in one place

Tools are grouped into hubs. Open any two or three side by side: drag a hub or a tab to the right of a pane, resize, and press ⌘K to jump anywhere. Every tab keeps its results until you reset it.

Data

Query, filter, export

SOQL with autocomplete, a table you can filter by any column, names for Ids, and exports of millions of rows with the Bulk API.

  • Saved queries and history
  • Click an Id to inspect and edit the record
  • CSV, JSON, or straight to the clipboard
Data protection

Backups you keep, restores that keep relationships

Choose objects, conditions and fields; back up to a folder on your computer, now or daily. Restore changed values or bring deleted records back — with their parents, their children re-pointed, and their files.

  • Incremental runs, deleted Ids, files and attachments
  • Optional AES-256 encryption with your passphrase
  • Compare any backup with the org before restoring
Data protection

Copy data between orgs

Seed a sandbox with a consistent slice: pick Accounts, add their child objects in one click, and NomadSF loads parents first and keeps every link. Mask personal data on the way.

  • A plan before anything is copied: order, counts, links
  • Re-runs update instead of duplicating; undo the last copy
  • Bulk mode for large volumes
Data protection

Lifecycle jobs with approval

Retention and clean-up in steps: back up, check a limit, archive, anonymize, delete. Dry runs only count; real runs stop for your approval before anything changes, and every run keeps a report.

Browser extension recommended

Chrome or Edge. Uses your browser's Salesforce login — no CLI, nothing stored.

Download .zip
  1. Unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Load unpacked → choose the unzipped folder.

Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons listings are on the way.

VS Code extension

The Studio in an editor tab, with your Salesforce DX project. Needs the Salesforce CLI.

Download .vsix
  1. VS Code → Extensions → … → Install from VSIX…
  2. Open a Salesforce DX project; click NomadSF in the status bar.
